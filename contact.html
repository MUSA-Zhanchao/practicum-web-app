About the project

MUSA Practicum · Spring 2026

This project is completed as part of the Master of Urban Spatial Analytics (MUSA) Practicum at the Weitzman School of Design, University of Pennsylvania.

The practicum pairs graduate students with real planning problems. Our partner challenge: build a scalable crossing-conditions inventory for Philadelphia without the cost of a city-wide field survey.
