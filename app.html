Explorer

Browse the detection layers by neighborhood.

Pick a neighborhood, toggle layers, and click a feature to inspect attributes and jump straight into Google Street View for visual verification.

1

Pick a neighborhood

Use the dropdown in the control panel. The map zooms to its detected intersections, traffic controls, and sidewalks.

2

Toggle layers

Show or hide sidewalks, intersections, traffic lights, and stop signs to focus on what's relevant for your question.

3

Click to verify

Click any point. The right-hand panel shows model attributes plus a Street View link to confirm before citing.
